Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Hermes Clips is a personal tool run by the owner of the connected YouTube channels. It uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to those channels and to read basic channel information (the channel name) to confirm which channel is connected.

Data we access

With your permission, the app receives an access token for your YouTube channel. It does not read your email, contacts, watch history or any other Google data.

How data is stored

The token is stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any other server or shared with third parties.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
